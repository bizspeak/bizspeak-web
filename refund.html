Refund Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Our Commitment

We want you to be satisfied with BizSpeak. If our VIP subscription doesn't meet your expectations, we offer a straightforward refund process.

Free Plan

The Free plan has no cost and requires no refund. You can use it indefinitely at no charge.

VIP Subscription Refunds

Within 7 days of first purchase

If you are not satisfied with your VIP subscription, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase. No questions asked.

After 7 days

After the initial 7-day period, refunds are generally not provided for the current billing period. However, we review each request on a case-by-case basis and may issue refunds in exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • Prolonged service outages that prevented you from using the Service
  • Billing errors or unauthorized charges
  • Significant feature changes that materially reduce the value of the subscription

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@bizspeak.app with:

  • Your account email address
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for the refund request

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 3 business days.

Cancellation

You can cancel your VIP subscription at any time from your account settings. When you cancel:

  • You retain VIP access until the end of your current billing period
  • No further charges will be made
  • Your account reverts to the Free plan after the billing period ends

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days. The refund will be issued to the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Contact

Questions about refunds? Contact us at support@bizspeak.app.
